      04 / Our Journey

      LAST YEAR AT A GLANCE

      A look back at the defining events and growth phases that shaped EPIC into the community it is today.

      Sept 2025 Foundation

      First Step Session

      EPIC's introductory session welcomes students into its technical community, covering EPIC's vision, domains, and team while giving beginners exposure to basic technologies and the opportunities EPIC offers.

      2025 Hackathon

      Internal SIH Session

      Organized by EPIC, this platform brought together 52 teams who pitched innovative solutions for the Smart India Hackathon before a panel of judges, showcasing innovation, problem-solving, and collaborative teamwork while identifying promising ideas for further development.

      2025 Cohort

      Google Cloud Study Jams

      Introduced students to Google Cloud through guided, hands-on learning. Participants explored cloud services, strengthened technical skills through structured practice, and received goodies upon successful completion.

      2026 Competition

      Study Pods 5.0

      Organized by EPIC, this platform brought together 52 teams who pitched innovative solutions for the Smart India Hackathon before a panel of judges, showcasing innovation, problem-solving, and collaborative teamwork while identifying promising ideas for further development.

      2026 Competition

      Atmanirbhar Bharat AI in Campus

      Tejasvini MBM: MBM University's national-level Campus AI Short Video Competition saw 50+ students participate across online and offline sessions at the CSE Department, with their creative takes on "AI in Campus Life" compiled into a final AICTE-submission video.

      2026 Hackathon

      Hack-a-Day

      A competitive 24-hour engineering challenge turning prompt problems into working applications across Web and AI tracks.

      2026 Competition

      Converge 2026

      A 3-week campus innovation sprint where teams turned raw ideas into working, AI-powered prototypes across tracks like Smart Campus, Environment, and Accessibility.

      10 / Frequently Asked Questions

      Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

      Everything you need to know about joining, contributing, and growing with EPIC.

      Any student from MBM University passionate about computing, software engineering, coding, or design is welcome! We welcome all years and all engineering disciplines.

      EPIC gives you hands-on coding experience, exposure to real-world projects, mentorship from seniors, and a community of like-minded engineers, whether you're just starting out or already competing at national hackathons.

      Not at all. Our SPARK branch is specifically tailored for absolute beginners, providing roadmaps, peer mentors, and first-step workshops to get you up to speed.

      Our members build full-stack web platforms, machine learning models, mobile apps, IoT systems, and algorithmic bots for national competitions and community impact.

      No, EPIC is completely free to join. We believe in accessible education and collaboration. All workshops, events, and mentorship sessions are open to all members at no cost.

      We cover a wide range: Web Development (React, Node.js, Python), AI/ML (TensorFlow, PyTorch), Cloud Computing (GCP, AWS), System Design, Competitive Programming, and more. Each wing has its own focus areas.
