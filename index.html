01 / Who We Are

Built by students.
Driven by innovation.

We bridge the gap between what you learn in class and what industry actually demands through projects, workshops, and challenges built to test real skills.

Whether you're writing your first line of code in SPARK, sharpening your engineering skills in KAIZEN, or turning your skills into income through PHOENIX, EPIC provides the mentorship, peers, and opportunities to help you excel.

      04 / Our Journey

      Milestones & History

      A look back at the defining events and growth phases that shaped EPIC into the community it is today.

      Sept 2025 Foundation

      First Step Session

      EPIC's introductory session welcomes students into its technical community, covering EPIC's vision, domains, and team while giving beginners exposure to basic technologies and the opportunities EPIC offers.

      2025 Hackathon

      Internal SIH Session

      A platform organized by EPIC where 52 teams pitched innovative solutions for the Smart India Hackathon before a panel of judges. It focused on innovation, problem-solving, teamwork, and identifying ideas for further development.

      2025 Cohort

      Google Cloud Study Jams

      Introduced students to Google Cloud through guided, hands-on learning. Participants explored cloud services, strengthened technical skills through structured practice, and received goodies upon successful completion.

      2026 Competition

      Study Pods 5.0

      A 4-week intensive sprint where teams built production systems in Frontend, Backend, AI/ML, and UI/UX, presenting live functional prototypes.

      2026 Hackathon

      Hack-a-Day

      A competitive 24-hour engineering challenge turning prompt problems into working applications across Web and AI tracks.

      2026 Competition

      Converge 2026

      A 3-week campus innovation sprint where teams turned raw ideas into working, AI-powered prototypes across tracks like Smart Campus, Environment, and Accessibility.
